# Product

## Users

People who run coding agents every day (Claude Code, Codex and the other
agents Conductor knows), several at once and often with Yolo on, in the
desktop app in a wide, short window (about 2000 by 810). They know their
agents, the terminal and git, and they come back to the same screens all
day. Others join a session or a run by a share link, from their own app or
a phone, and answer what an agent asks while away from their desk.

## Product Principles

1. What people use all the time stays in view, one press away, under the
   name they already use (Yolo, Accounts). How often a thing is used
   decides whether it folds away, never how technical it looks.
2. Simpler means less to watch and less to hold in the head, never more
   presses for daily work: one idea a screen, few things on it, plain
   words.
3. The views where a person works with an agent, Chat and Terminal, come
   first.
4. A settings page folds nothing: its settings are the page. It is made
   calmer by plainer words and a better order.
5. Nowhere to get lost: a crew's member opens inside its run, with the way
   back in view and in words.

## Product Purpose

Conductor runs coding agents in real terminals on a machine you choose,
shows them in its apps, and lets other people watch or drive a live
session through a link. Yardmaster, its agent harness, runs a crew of
agents with tasks as a managed run; Dispatch tracks the tickets the work is
about. It succeeds when a person keeps many agents moving with little to
watch: what needs them is in front of them and said once, and nothing is
done in their name that they did not allow.

## Brand Commitments

- The visual system is `DESIGN.md` (generated from the code), its rules
  are `docs/design/system.md` and the brand is `docs/design/brand.md`.
  Keep Inter, JetBrains Mono for evidence, and the forest, ink and
  terracotta palette; refine inside them rather than propose a new visual
  direction.
- Calm, precise, plain engineering words; railroad names only for the
  parts: Conductor, Yardmaster, Dispatch, Brakeman, Switchman.
- Conductor is used in its apps, from anywhere; its words never say
  "browser" for that.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every text meets its contrast floor in both themes; a state is never told
by colour alone; an icon-only button has a label that is both its tooltip
and its accessible name; on a phone, touch targets are at least 44 px and
nothing scrolls sideways (`docs/design/system.md`). These are the
project's own checks, not a certification.
